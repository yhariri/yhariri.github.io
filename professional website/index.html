Profile

About Me

Yassine Hariri is a PhD electrical engineer and technology leader with over 17 years at the intersection of semiconductor hardware, embedded intelligence, and national research infrastructure. Currently serving as Manager of IoT & Edge AI in the CTO Office at CMC Microsystems, Ottawa, he sits at the convergence of Canada's national chip design ecosystem and the applied AI deployment frontier.

His technical foundation is deep and wide — from ASIC tape-out flows (RTL → synthesis → place & route → DRC/LVS, with Synopsys, Cadence, and Mentor toolchains) and FPGA/SoC design (Xilinx/AMD, VHDL, HLS, radiation-tolerant architectures) to edge AI inference on NVIDIA Jetson platforms using TensorRT and YOLOv9. His research roots are in fault diagnostics (ΔIDDQ, ATPG), SRAM soft-error analysis, and MEMS RF switch characterization.

Beyond engineering, Yassine leads national industry-academia-government programs, mentors deep-tech startups through AI commercialization, and curates hands-on education through his Accelerating AI workshop series. He also runs a YouTube channel on IoT & Edge AI, building a public engineering knowledge commons — in English, French, and Arabic.
